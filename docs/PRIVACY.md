# Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Auxo is a point-of-sale app that runs on your phone. This page says what it does with information.

## Your shop's data stays on your phone
Products, bills, customers, stock and settings are stored in an encrypted database on your device. Auxo has no server and no account, and the developers cannot see your data. Backups you create are files you keep and move yourself. Every backup is encrypted with a passphrase you choose, which Auxo cannot recover, and is saved in the Auxo folder in Documents on your phone.

## What goes over the network
- **Update check.** When the app opens, and when you tap Check for updates, it asks GitHub (`api.github.com`) for the latest release of this project. The request contains no shop or personal data. GitHub may log standard request information, such as your IP address, under [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
- **Nothing else.** There is no analytics, advertising or tracking.

## Crash and problem reports
If the app crashes, it saves a short report on your phone. It is sent only if you choose to share it. A report holds the app and phone version, the error and the names of recent screens. It holds no customer, sales or shop details, and database keys, email addresses and long numbers are removed. If you choose **Report on GitHub**, the text you submit becomes a public issue on this repository.

## Permissions
Internet (update check), install unknown apps (installing an update you downloaded), Bluetooth connect (receipt printers), and storage on Android 9 and older (saving backups, including automatic ones). Camera scanning uses Google Play services' scanner, which is governed by Google's policies.
